About me

From fixing school computers in 2012 to architecting Power Platform and Azure solutions today.

Bio

I'm a technical consultant at eSolutions Consulting Ghana. My mission is simple: replace manual processes with efficient electronic systems, so teams stop re-typing, chasing signatures and losing paper.

I design and build Power Apps, Power Automate flows, Power BI dashboards and Azure-backed applications, and I train client teams so the tools actually get used. I also mentor computer engineering interns and enjoy teaching young people to think logically.
